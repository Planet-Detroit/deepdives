  \- \*\*2026 heat NEP:\*\* has \*\*no numerical inspection goals\*\*, which he views as a sign it will be less effective

  \- OSHA must prove \*\*4 elements\*\* to cite under the general duty clause:  
    1\. There is a \*\*hazard\*\*    
    2\. The hazard is \*\*serious\*\*    
    3\. It is a \*\*recognized\*\* hazard    
    4\. There is a \*\*feasible means of abatement\*\*    
  \- He describes this route as \*\*burdensome, resource-intensive, and vulnerable to legal challenge\*\*
