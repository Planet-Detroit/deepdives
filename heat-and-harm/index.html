Investigative Series

Heat & Harm

As the climate crisis accelerates, who bears the cost? This investigative series examines how rising temperatures are reshaping the lives of workers across Michigan — with a focus on Detroit's Latino community, where outdoor laborers face dangerous heat with few protections.
